The Oracles

Oracular Spectacular

Three eight-sided dice; five hundred and twelve sayings of the ancients, from many lands.

Oblique Tarot

Three cards from a Marseille deck, each with a terse prompt for the writer, painter, sculptor or composer.

The Book of Changes, cast with fifty yarrow stalks. Sixty-four hexagrams, with the original verse and a modern one.
